# ASNF Techno Lab Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
As members, contributors, and leaders of the **ASNF Techno Lab** and **AnXin Technology Project** ecosystems, we pledge to foster an environment of professional respect, technological innovation, and structural integrity. We welcome users from all devices and environments, and we are dedicated to maximizing performance for everyone.

## Standards of Engagement
We encourage positive behaviors within our code repositories, discussion boards, and community spaces:
- Demonstrating professional respect for intellectual property rights.
- Providing constructive feedback regarding UI scaling or module performance.
- Respecting the proprietary guidelines set forth by the company management.
- Showing empathy and kindness towards fellow developers and testers.

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Plagiarism, unauthorized snipping (lsnip), or copying engine structures without explicit authorization from the founder.
- Defacing repository integrity, spamming, or attacking the community infrastructure.
- Publicly exploiting bugs or vulnerabilities instead of using secure reporting channels.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
The company management, led by Founder and CEO **Muhammad Nabil Irza Prawira**, holds full authority to moderate community spaces, address violations, and revoke repository access tokens if this Code of Conduct or the software licensing agreement is compromised.
